PRODUCT SPECIFICATION
What is a flexitank and what can it carry?

A flexitank is a high-strength flexible bulk-liquid bladder fitted inside a standard 20ft ISO ocean container, carrying up to ~26,000 litres of non-hazardous bulk liquids. Typical cargos include industrial chemicals, base oils, engine lubricants, edible oils, wine, juice concentrates, food-grade glycerine, natural rubber latex, and dairy liquids.

COST & FREIGHT EFFICIENCY
How does flexitank cost compare to drums or ISO tanks?

Flexitanks deliver a 15% to 30% lower per-litre freight cost compared to 200L steel drums by eliminating palletizing, strapping, and tare weight penalties. Unlike ISO tank containers, flexitanks require no empty repositioning freight or expensive chemical washing fees after discharge.

GLOBAL QUALITY AUDIT
What quality certifications does BPS Flexitank hold?

Every product is manufactured under accredited ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management), ISO 14001:2015 (Environmental Management), ISO 22000:2018 (Food Safety), and HACCP systems. Raw materials are certified 100% Heavy Metal Free (Pb, Cd, Hg, Cr < 100ppm) and Phthalate Free.

FOOD-GRADE APPROVALS
Is BPS Flexitank approved for food and beverage cargo?

Yes — fully compliant with U.S. FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 and EU Commission Regulation 10/2011 for direct food contact. Tested and approved by SGS and Japan Food Research Laboratories, alongside official Kosher and Halal certifications.

TRANSPORT SAFETY & RAIL IMPACT
What transport and structural safety standards apply?

BPS Flexitanks comply strictly with British Standards BSI PAS 1008:2016 and the COA Recommended Code of Practice. They have passed mandatory dynamic rail-impact tests witnessed and certified by Lloyd's Register, earning the COA Quality Conformance (CQC) approval for worldwide intermodal rail, road, and maritime shipping.

INDUSTRY MEMBERSHIP
Is BPS a member of any recognised industry bodies?

Yes — BPS is an active corporate member of the Container Owners Association (COA) and the World Cargo Alliance (WCAworld), reflecting our commitment to the highest global industrial safety benchmarks, operational transparency, and standardized equipment integrity.

STORAGE & SHELF LIFE
What is the shelf life and storage requirement?

Unfilled BPS Flexitanks have an operating shelf life of 12 to 24 months when stored in original protective packaging in a clean, dry, shaded environment at temperatures between 5°C and 35°C. Stock should be rotated on a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) basis and inspected before container fitting.

CONTAINER INSPECTION
What container conditions are unsuitable for flexitanks?

Containers with structural distortion, sharp burrs, protruding nails or screws, chemical floor stains, damaged locking gear, or payload rating under 30,480 kg (67,200 lbs) are strictly unsuitable. All containers must undergo our certified 9-point container preparation checklist before liner installation.

CUSTOM ENGINEERING
Can flexitanks be customised for specific cargo needs?

Yes — configurations can be tailored for specialized payloads: bottom or top loading/discharge, 2" or 3" ball/butterfly valves with Camlock or quick couplings, high-temperature heat-resistant films (up to 90°C), integrated oxygen barrier EVOH films, anti-wave baffles, and external steam heating pads.
